    This Is Fast · 24 Hour One-Color Screen Print

    Set up your artwork

    Upload one-color vector art, place it, pick your PMS ink, and see the finished product before you submit.
    Watch the workflow
    Artwork requirements
    One-color vector art only
    Production-ready vector artwork — AI, EPS, PDF or SVG. Photos, JPGs and PNGs can't be screen printed.
    12 pt minimum type
    All fonts and type must be 12 pt or larger so they stay legible once printed.
    Strokes greater than 1 pt
    Fine lines below 1 pt can drop out or fill in on press. Thicken them before uploading.
    Transparent background
    No white or colored background box. Anything behind your design is treated as part of the printed artwork.
    Five steps
    1
    Upload your artwork
    One-color vector art with a transparent background. We check the file as it uploads and tell you if something needs fixing.
    2
    Position your design
    Drag to move, then scale and rotate to fit inside the approved imprint area.
    3
    Choose your PMS color
    Pick the screen-print ink from the approved PMS library. Your artwork recolors instantly — the shape never changes.
    4
    Preview your product
    Switch to Proof to see a realistic mockup with your product options, placement and ink color.
    5
    Review & submit
    Confirm artwork, placement, product options and PMS color, then submit your configuration.
    You can reopen this any time with ? Tutorial above the product view.
